Why HINT?

We believe no young African should be invisible. HINT empowers youth to own their identity, control their data, and access opportunities that can transform their lives. From Nairobi to Lagos, Kigali to Accra – HINT is designed to be inclusive, secure, and future-ready.

Our Impact Goals

  • 50% increase in verified youth employment within 2 years.
  • Alternative credit access for 100,000 youth by 2027.
  • Recognition for millions of informal workers, freelancers, and creators.
  • Regional expansion across Africa in partnership with governments and organizations.
