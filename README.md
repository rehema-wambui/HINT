## 📌 Project Overview

**Digital Passport for Business Identity** is a web-based platform designed to establish, verify, and present official business identities in a secure and structured format. The platform provides a centralized digital credential system that helps individuals or businesses showcase their verified organizational details, operational legitimacy, and compliance status.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Digital Identity Verification:** Creates a standardized digital passport for registered businesses.
- **Streamlined Credential Sharing:** Allows organizations to easily share verified profiles with clients, partners, and regulatory bodies.
- **Structured Business Details:** Displays core company metadata, registration status, and official credentials in a clear layout.
- **Modern Web Interface:** Accessible online for seamless cross-platform identity management.
